PINOT COUNTRY — WRITING A RECORD
================================

One JSON file per node, in data/nodes/<type>/<id>.json. The schema is
schema/node.schema.json. Copy a record close to what you are writing and change every
field. Run  python3 tools/validate.py  until it passes.

TYPES
  variety   the vine and its mutations      region    an appellation, AVA, valley, commune
  vineyard  a named site or cru             producer  a domaine, winery, tasting room
  clone     a numbered or named selection   practice  what happens in vineyard or cellar
  pairing   what gets eaten beside it       person    grower, winemaker, scientist, writer
  org       an appellation body, a nursery  event     an auction, a celebration
  term      a word, with its root           art       labels, posters, photographs
  story     a long read, in sections

FIELDS THAT CARRY THE WEIGHT
  names.name     what the trade calls it. names.said = how it is said aloud.
  text.what      one plain paragraph. Present tense. What it is.
  text.story     narrative, told as narrative. Scenes, dates, names. Mark each stretch:
                   plain prose          = cited in this record's sources
                   *Tradition holds —*  general knowledge of the trade, hedged
                   *Inference —*        this project's own reasoning
  text.how       how it is grown, pressed, aged, served. text.today = now, dated.
  kin[]          what another node IS to this one, said from here. One sentence a reader
                 standing on this page wants — not "see also" but the fact that joins
                 them ("the 1.8-hectare monopole at the centre of the argument"). The
                 other record answers in its own words, and the site prints both.
  etymology      required on type=term, welcome anywhere a name has a root.
                 first_attested = a date with a source behind it, else blank.
  facets         open keys per type, see data/vocab/facets.json: country, level, soil,
                 established, kind, course, role, status, origin, letter, colour.
  sources[]      ids from data/sources/sources.json ONLY. Add new ones to
                 data/sources/new-<name>.json in the same shape; build.py merges them.
                 Wikipedia: s:wp-<slug>, and the registry keeps the revision permalink.
  provenance     default tier + per-field overrides. Tiers: cited, harvested, tradition,
                 inference, field. A date, a hectare count, a founding year, a price:
                 cite it or hedge it.
  geo / address  coordinates and precision. address.country takes ISO 3166-1 alpha-2.
  images[]       leave empty. Put candidate Commons titles in x_commons_files, then run
                 tools/harvest_commons.py --harvest <id> --apply.
  confidence     high / medium / low. needs_verification: true when a fact stays unchecked.

MEASURES — numbers somebody else published
  measures[] {key, label, value, unit, year, source, url, tier, note}. Planted area,
  elevation, a harvest date, a bottle count. EVERY row names its own source. Nothing in
  this array is estimated, averaged or inferred; if the number is not published, the row
  is not written. The /numbers/ page and the cards read straight from here.

TAGS — claims a reader might choose by
  tags[] {tag, source, tier?, note?, since?, url?}. Keys in data/vocab/tags.json, and
  EVERY tag names its evidence. Ownership, welcome and certification tags come only from
  the owner's own words, a press profile naming them, a certifier, or a public register —
  never from a name, a photograph or a hunch. The validator refuses tier tradition or
  inference on a tag. No evidence, no tag; a missing tag says nothing about the cellar.

HOURS — three states, never two
  hours{open[], closed[], text, sold_out, seasonal, source, url, tier, checked}.
  A day in `open` is open. A day in `closed` is closed. A day in NEITHER is not known,
  and is drawn dashed and excluded from the filters. Guessing a closure is worse than a
  blank, because a reader drives on it. The validator refuses a day in both lists.

RECOGNITIONS — attention paid, not our ranking
  recognitions[] {by, what, year, source, url}. Recognizers in data/vocab/recognizers.json:
  an appellation established by INAO, an AVA established in 27 CFR part 9, a UNESCO
  listing. The count drives what the front page shows; it is never a verdict.

RECIPES
  recipes[] — public domain or CC BY-SA copied in full with attribution; anything else
  license: "ingredients-only", and then text must be empty. A list of ingredients is a
  fact. Somebody's method is not.

THE VOICE
  Active verbs. Fewer adjectives. Say the finding, do not name the chart. Describe;
  never adjudicate. The banned list in tools/validate.py includes the fleet's fluff
  words and this subject's adjudicating vocabulary — authentic, purist, world-class,
  iconic, must-visit, overrated, wine snob. If somebody said it, quote them and name
  them; otherwise it does not go in.
