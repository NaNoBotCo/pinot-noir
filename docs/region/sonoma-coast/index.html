regionSonoma Coast

An AVA big enough to be an argument: it runs from the Pacific bluffs inland across most of the county. The vines that gave it its name sit on the far western ridges, cold and steep, and growers there spent years carving out smaller names.

Written down by US Alcohol and Tobacco Tax and Trade Bureau (1987)

Today Cited

Fort Ross-Seaview came out of it in 2011, West Sonoma Coast in 2022 — both drawn tighter and closer to the ocean.
