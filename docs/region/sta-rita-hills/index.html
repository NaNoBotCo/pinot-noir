regionSta. Rita Hills

also: Santa Rita Hills

A wind tunnel between Buellton and Lompoc where the Santa Ynez Valley opens west to the ocean. Fog in the morning, thirty-knot afternoons, and some of the slowest ripening in California.

Written down by US Alcohol and Tobacco Tax and Trade Bureau (2001)

The story Cited

Petitioned by growers inside the Santa Ynez Valley AVA who wanted their own name, and established as Santa Rita Hills in 2001. Chile's Viña Santa Rita objected; the TTB shortened it to Sta. Rita Hills in 2005, and the label now carries an abbreviation nobody says out loud.
