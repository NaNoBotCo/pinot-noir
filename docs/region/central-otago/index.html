regionCentral Otago

The southernmost commercial wine region on earth, and the only part of New Zealand with a true continental climate — frost in the spring, forty-degree swings in a day, schist under everything.

The story Cited

Vines went from 92 hectares to 1,930 in about two decades. Pinot noir takes over 80% of the plantings, which is a bet no other region of this size has made.
