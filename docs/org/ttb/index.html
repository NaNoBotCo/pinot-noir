also: Alcohol and Tobacco Tax and Trade Bureau

The US Alcohol and Tobacco Tax and Trade Bureau: it rules on AVA petitions, writes them into 27 CFR part 9, and approves every label that goes on a bottle.

How it is done Cited

An AVA petition has to show the name is already used for the place, propose a boundary, and show the growing conditions differ from the ground around it. Nothing in the rule speaks to quality.
