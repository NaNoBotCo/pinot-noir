an organizationSanta Maria Style Barbeque Association

The body at the Santa Maria Valley Chamber of Commerce that defines the local barbecue and certifies places serving it: beef rolled in salt, pepper and garlic, cooked on skewers over coals of red oak native to the valley.

Today Cited

Its page points readers to 'Santa Maria Style Barbeque Certified' locations and invites places to get certified; the criteria themselves were not published on the page this project read on 16 September 2026.
