an organizationFoundation Plant Services

also: Foundation Plant Services, UC Davis · FPS

The UC Davis programme that holds, tests and distributes certified grapevine material in the United States. The numbers on American clones — UCD 4, UCD 2A — are its numbers.

How it is done Cited

Imported vines go through quarantine and virus testing before release. That process is why certified material is slow and expensive, and why suitcase cuttings keep happening.
